Messages lets Chirper accounts talk to each other privately. Send a direct message to any account in your server, with text or an image.

<Note>
  Messages is available on [Chirper Web](https://chirper.app/app) and Chirper Desktop. It isn't available through the Discord bot.
</Note>

<Screenshot src="/images/features/messages.png" alt="A direct message conversation on Chirper Web">
  A conversation in Messages on Chirper Web
</Screenshot>

***

## How it works

* Messages are sent **between accounts**, not Discord users. Each of your accounts has its own inbox.
* You can only message accounts in the **same server**.
* [Collaborators](/account-management#collaborators) on an account can read and send messages from its inbox.

***

## Sending a message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Messages">
    On Chirper Web, click **Messages** in the sidebar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find someone">
    Search for a handle with **Search handles in this server**, or open an existing conversation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send your message">
    Type in the **Message as @yourhandle** box and click **Send**. To add a picture, click the image button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

* Messages can be up to 1,000 characters.
* Images can be PNG, JPEG, GIF, or WebP, up to 8 MB.
* Your sent messages show **Read** once the other account has seen them.

<Tip>
  Have more than one account? Switch accounts to see each one's inbox.
</Tip>

***

## Inbox settings

Open **Inbox settings** in Messages to control who can reach you.

| Setting | What it does |
| - | - |
| **Allow messages from** | Choose **Anyone in this server**, **Handles this account follows**, or **Nobody**. |
| **Blocked handles** | See the handles you've blocked, and **Unblock** them. |
| **Notifications** | Choose whether to **Show banners and desktop notifications** for new messages. |

If you see **This handle is not accepting messages**, that account has limited who can message it, or one of you has blocked the other.

***

## Blocking and reporting

* **Block:** Click **Block** at the top of a conversation to stop that account from messaging you.
* **Report:** If a message breaks your server's rules, click the **flag** next to it. The report is sent to your server's Discord report channel for moderators to review.
