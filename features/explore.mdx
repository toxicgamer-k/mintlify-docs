## Searching

Click **Explore** in the sidebar, or use the **Search Chirper** bar at the top of the right-hand sidebar. Type what you're looking for.

Results are split into two groups:

| Group | What it finds |
| - | - |
| **People** | Accounts whose handle matches your search. |
| **Chirps** | Posts that contain your search. Replies aren't included. |

<Tip>
  You can start a search with `@` or `#`. Searching `@john` finds the same people as `john`, and `#event` finds posts that mention it.
</Tip>

Search only looks at your current server. To search another server, switch servers first.

***

## Recent searches

Explore remembers your last 5 searches. Click one to run it again, or click **Clear** to remove them.

***

## Who to follow

Before you search, and in the sidebar on most pages, Explore suggests accounts under **Who to follow**. Click **Follow** to follow one with your current account.

See [Following](/features/internet#following) for more on followers.
