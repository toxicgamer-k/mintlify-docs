Chirper Internet covers the core social media features of Chirper. Share news, updates, events, and announcements, then reply, like, and repost to keep the conversation going.

Posts sync live between Discord, [Chirper Web](https://chirper.app/app), and Chirper Desktop.

## Before you start

You need a Chirper account to post and interact. If you already have one, skip ahead to [Making a post](#making-a-post).

Run `/account register` in your server's bot commands channel and fill in your details.

| Field | Details |
| - | - |
| **Handle** | Your unique `@handle`. |
| **Display Name** | The name shown on your profile and posts. |
| **Profile Picture** | Optional. |
| **Biography** | Optional. A short description for your profile. |
| **Theme** | Optional. Customize how your profile and posts look. |

See [Account management](/account-management) for more on accounts.

## Making a post

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Discord">

        | Field | Details |

        | **Tags** | Optional. Pick up to 5 tags from your server's forum. They show as hashtags on your post. |
        | **Content** | Up to 1,000 characters. |
        | **Image** | Optional. PNG, JPEG, GIF, or WebP. |
      </Step>

      <Step title="Submit">
        Click **Submit**. Your post is published to the **Chirper Posts** channel.
      </Step>

### After posting

In Discord, Chirper sends you a private confirmation that only you can see:

> **@you**, I've posted as **@handle** — your post is live!

Click **View post** to jump straight to it, or click **Dismiss message** and find it in your server's **Chirper Posts** channel.

<Tip>
  Mention another account with its `@handle`. Chirper sends them a DM letting them know.
</Tip>

You can post again from the same account after 10 seconds.

## Polls

Ask a question and let everyone vote.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run /post">
    Run `/post` and choose **Poll**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in your poll">
    | Field | Details |
    | - | - |
    | **Account** | Which of your accounts to post from. |
    | **Question** | Up to 1,000 characters. |
    | **Options** | 2 to 4 options, one per line. Each can be up to 50 characters. |
    | **Duration** | 1 hour, 6 hours, 24 hours, 3 days, or 7 days. |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit** to publish your poll.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Put each option on its own line. Press **Enter** to start the next one. For example, for **"Should we host a community event?"**:

```text
Yes
No
Maybe
```

### Voting

Polls show live results as a percentage for each option, along with the total votes and when the poll ends.

## Interacting with posts

Every post has buttons along the bottom. The number on each button shows its current count.

<Screenshot src="/images/features/internet/post-buttons.png" alt="The Reply, Repost, Like, Bookmark, and more options buttons under a post" width={300}>
  Reply, Repost, Like, Bookmark, and More options
</Screenshot>

<Info>
  If you have more than one account, Chirper asks which account to use in a private message only you can see. Click **Dismiss message** to clear it when you're done. With one account, Chirper uses it automatically.
</Info>

### Replying

Click the **speech bubble** button on any post, including your own and Dark Web posts. Pick the **Account** to reply with, write your reply in **Content**, and optionally add an **Image**, then submit.

On Chirper Web, open a post and use the **Chirp your reply** box at the bottom.

### Liking

Click the **heart** button to like a post. Click it again to remove your like.

### Reposting

Click the **two looping arrows** to share a post from your account. Chirper adds a message to the post's thread saying you reposted it.

Reposts help posts get seen:

* Depending on your server's settings, a post that gets enough reposts moves back to the top of the [Latest Feed](#feeds).
* A post that reaches your server's repost threshold is also sent to the [Trending Feed](#feeds).

### Bookmarking

Click the **bookmark** button to save a post to your account's bookmarks.

To see your saved posts, run `/bookmarks`. If you have bookmarks on more than one account, pick which account's bookmarks to view. Use the **arrow** buttons to move through them.

| Button | What it does |
| - | - |
| **View post** | Open the original post. |
| **Delete** | Remove the bookmark. |

## More options

Click `...` on a post to open a private menu, then pick an action from **What would you like to do?**. The actions you see depend on whose post it is.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Your posts">

    | - | - |
    | **Edit** | Change your post. |

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Other posts">

    | - | - |
    | **View Profile** | Open the author's profile. |
    | **Follow** | Follow the account. See [Following](#following). |

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Following

Follow an account to get a DM whenever it posts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open more options">
    Click `...` on one of the account's posts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Follow">
    Choose **Follow**, then pick which of your accounts to follow with. Chirper always asks, even if you only have one account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The account you follow gets a notification that you followed them. To unfollow, repeat the same steps and choose **Unfollow**. They aren't notified when you unfollow.

***

## Anonymous posting

Post or reply without showing which account you are. Choose **@anonymous** from the **Account** menu when you post or reply. Anonymous posting works for both Internet and Dark Web posts.

* You need at least one Chirper account to post anonymously.
* Your server admins can turn anonymous posting off.

<Note>
  Server staff can still see who made an anonymous post or reply, so they can moderate it if needed.
</Note>

## Notifications

Chirper keeps you in the loop with DMs.

| Notification | When you get it |
| - | - |
| **Mentions** | Someone mentions one of your accounts in a post or reply. |
| **New posts** | An account you follow publishes a new post. |
| **New followers** | Someone follows one of your accounts. |

Choose which DMs you receive with `/notifications manage`.

***

## Reporting

If a post needs moderator attention, report it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open more options">
    Click `...` on the post.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Report">
    Choose **Report**, enter your reason, and submit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your report is sent to your server's moderators to review. You can send another report after 2 minutes.

***

## Dark Web

Dark Web posts are built for underground and criminal roleplay. To make one, run `/post` and choose **Dark Web**. You can reply to and like Dark Web posts, but you can't repost them or bookmark them in Discord.

See [Dark Web](/features/dark-web) for everything else.
