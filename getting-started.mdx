<Note>
  This page is for **server admins** who want to add Chirper to their Discord server. If you just want to use Chirper, head to [Features](/features/overview).
</Note>

This guide walks you through inviting Chirper to your Discord server and completing the initial setup. Whether you're setting up Chirper for the first time or configuring it for a new server, each step gets everything ready for your community.

By the end of this guide, Chirper will be fully configured and ready for your members to use.

<Info>
  **Before you begin**, make sure you have permission to manage your Discord server. You need it to invite Chirper, enable Community, and create roles and channels.
</Info>

## Step 1 — Invite Chirper

Before you can configure Chirper, you need to invite the bot to your Discord server.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Invite Chirper" icon="discord" href="https://chirper.app/invite">
    Add the Chirper bot to your Discord server.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chirper website" icon="globe" href="https://chirper.app">
    Learn more about Chirper and access your dashboard.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your server">
    Select the server you want to add Chirper to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Chirper">
    Review the permissions Chirper requests, then continue through Discord's authorization process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Chirper joined">
    Return to your Discord server and check the member list to confirm that Chirper has successfully joined.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2 — Enable Community

Chirper uses **forum channels** for its posts, and Discord only allows forum channels on servers with **Community** enabled.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Server Settings">
    Open your Discord server, then open **Server Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Enable Community">
    Navigate to **Enable Community**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Discord's setup">
    Follow Discord's setup process and complete the required steps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once Community is enabled, you can create the forum channel that Chirper needs.

<Note>
  Discord may change the location or wording of its settings as the platform is updated.
</Note>

## Step 3 — Create the required roles and channels

Create the roles and channels Chirper will use. These are required for a basic setup.

### Roles

You need at least one **Manager Role** and one **Moderator Role**.

| Role | Minimum | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manager Role | 1 role | Lets selected members manage your server's Chirper settings. |
| Moderator Role | 1 role | Lets selected members moderate Chirper posts, reports, and profiles. |

<Tip>
  Already have staff roles? You can use your existing roles instead of creating new ones. You can also select multiple roles for both the Manager Role and Moderator Role during configuration.
</Tip>

### Channels

| Channel | Type | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audit Logs | Text channel | Receives Chirper audit logs. |
| Report Channel | Text channel | Receives Chirper reports. |
| Post Channel | **Forum channel** | Where Chirper posts are published. |

<Warning>
  Your Post Channel **must be a forum channel**. If you don't see the forum option when creating a channel, make sure you enabled Community in Step 2.
</Warning>

## Step 4 — Open the Chirper dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run setup">
    Go to your bot commands channel and run `/setup`. This opens a setup interface containing a **Dashboard** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Click **Dashboard** to open your server's Chirper dashboard. You'll land on the **Settings** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The left-hand side of the **Settings** page lists the sections you'll configure in the next step: **Roles**, **Logging**, **Forums**, **Feeds**, and **Misc**.

## Step 5 — Configure your settings

Work through each section of the **Settings** page. Expand a section below to see what to set.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Roles" icon="user-shield" defaultOpen>
    | Setting | What to select |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Manager Role** | The role or roles that can manage your server's Chirper settings. |
    | **Moderator Role** | The role or roles that can moderate Chirper posts, reports, and profiles. |

    You can select multiple roles for both settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Logging" icon="scroll">
    | Setting | What to select |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Audit Logs** | The channel you created for Chirper audit logs. |
    | **Report Channel** | The channel you created for receiving Chirper reports. |
    | **Report Ping Role** *(optional)* | The role or roles to notify whenever a new report is submitted. |
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Forums" icon="comments">
    This section determines where Chirper posts are sent.

    | Setting | What to select |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Chirper Posts** | The forum channel you created for Chirper posts. |
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Feeds" icon="rss">
    The **Feeds** section contains additional feed-related options. For a basic setup, you don't need to change anything here unless your server specifically requires it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Misc" icon="sliders">
    | Setting | What to select |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Server Timezone** | The timezone your server uses. Chirper uses it when displaying timestamps on posts. |
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Save your changes

When you've completed the required settings, click the orange **Save Changes** button.

<Check>
  **Your basic Chirper setup is complete!** Return to your bot commands channel and send your first post with `/post` to verify that Chirper is configured correctly and working as expected.
</Check>

<Tip>
  The settings above are the basic configuration needed to run Chirper. You can return to the dashboard at any time to configure other features and further customize how Chirper works in your community.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Chirper isn't in my member list" icon="user-xmark">
    Make sure you selected the correct server during the invite and completed Discord's authorization process. If Chirper still isn't there, [invite it again](https://chirper.app/invite).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My post channel doesn't appear under Chirper Posts" icon="comments">
    The **Chirper Posts** setting only accepts a **forum channel**. Check that your Post Channel was created as a forum channel, and that Community is enabled on your server.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My first post isn't showing up" icon="circle-exclamation">
    Open the dashboard again and confirm you clicked **Save Changes**, and that **Chirper Posts** is set to your forum channel. To post, you also need a Chirper account. Create one with `/account register`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
