---
title: "Install on your phone"
description: "Add Chirper to your Home Screen so it opens like an app."
icon: "mobile-screen-button"
---

Add Chirper Web to your phone's Home Screen and it opens like a regular app, full screen and without the browser bar.

<Note>
  **On iPhone and iPad, this is required.** Chirper Web only works on iOS after you add it to your Home Screen.
</Note>

***

## Installing

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone and iPad">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Chirper in Safari">
        Go to [chirper.app/app](https://chirper.app/app) in **Safari**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Tap Share">
        Tap **Share** in Safari's toolbar. Don't see it? Tap **⋯** first.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Add to Home Screen">
        Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Open Chirper">
        Open **Chirper** from your Home Screen and sign in with Discord.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Android">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Chirper in Chrome">
        Go to [chirper.app/app](https://chirper.app/app) in **Chrome**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Open the menu">
        Tap the **⋮** menu in the top-right corner.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Install">
        Tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**, then confirm.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Open Chirper">
        Open **Chirper** from your Home Screen and sign in with Discord.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

***

## After installing

The installed app works the same as [Chirper Web](/apps/chirper-web). Everything syncs live with your server's Discord.
