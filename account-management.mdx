## How accounts work

* Accounts belong to **one server**. An account you make in one server doesn't exist in another.
* You can have up to **3 accounts** per server, so you can run more than one character.
* Every account has a unique `@handle` within the server.

<Tip>
  Boost the [Chirper support server](https://chirper.app/support) to raise your limit to **5 accounts** per server.
</Tip>

## Creating an account

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Discord">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Run /account register">
        Run `/account register` in your server's bot commands channel. The **Account Registration** form opens.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Fill in your details">
        | Field | Details |
        | - | - |
        | **Handle** | Your unique `@username`. 2 to 20 characters. |
        | **Display Name** | The name shown on your profile and posts. Up to 40 characters. |
        | **Profile Picture** | Optional. |
        | **Biography** | Optional. Up to 500 characters. |
        | **Theme** | Optional. The color of your profile and posts. |
      </Step>

      <Step title="Submit">
        Click **Submit**. Your account is ready to use.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Screenshot src="/images/account-management/account-register.png" alt="The Account Registration form with Handle, Display Name, and Profile Picture fields" width={300}>
      The Account Registration form
    </Screenshot>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Chirper Web">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open New account">
        On [Chirper Web](https://chirper.app/app), open the **New account** page.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Fill in your details">
        Enter your **Handle**, **Display name**, and an optional **Bio**. Click the camera to add a profile picture. If you don't add one, Chirper uses your Discord avatar.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Create your account">
        Click **Create account**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      Already have a Chirper account in another server? Use **Quick Setup** to copy that profile instead of filling everything in again.
    </Tip>

    <Screenshot src="/images/account-management/account-register-web.png" alt="The New account page on Chirper Web with Handle, Display name, Bio, and Quick Setup">
      Creating an account on Chirper Web
    </Screenshot>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Your server can block certain words from handles and display names.

## Viewing profiles

Run `/profile` and pick a handle to view any account in the server. Leave it empty to view your own. If you have more than one account, Chirper asks which one.

You can also open a profile from a post. Click `...` on the post and choose **View Profile**.

On [Chirper Web](https://chirper.app/app), click a name or avatar anywhere, or click **Profile** in the sidebar to see your own.

Profiles show the account's following and follower counts, number of posts, bio, and when it joined. On Chirper Web, you can also browse its **Chirps**, **Replies**, **Media**, and **Likes**.

## Editing your account

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Discord">

## Collaborators

Collaborators can post as your account. Use them for shared accounts, like a business or a department.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite someone">
    Run `/collaborator add`, pick your account, and choose a member of the server.
  </Step>

  <Step title="They accept">
    Chirper sends them a DM with **Accept** and **Decline** buttons. Once they accept, they can post as your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove a collaborator, or cancel an invite they haven't accepted yet, run `/collaborator remove`.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Collaborator limits">
    * An account can have up to **10** collaborators, including pending invites.
    * The person you invite must be a member of the server.
    * Only the account's owner can add or remove collaborators.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Collaborators on Chirper Web">
    On [Chirper Web](https://chirper.app/app), open **Settings** and go to **Collaborators** to see who can post as your account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Transferring ownership

Give an account to someone else, like when you hand a business over to a new owner.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Transfer Ownership">
    Run `/profile`, open your own profile, and click **Settings** → **Transfer Ownership**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the new owner">
    Choose the member who should receive the account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    Check the details and click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Transferring ownership **can't be undone**. The new owner gets the account's followers, posts, and everything tied to it, and you no longer own it.
</Warning>

## Badges

Badges show next to an account's name on profiles and posts.

<Warning>
  **Badges are server-specific.** Each server's managers decide who gets a badge in their server. A badge in one server doesn't carry over to any other server.

  **Chirper staff will not grant you a badge in any server.** Don't open a support ticket asking for one. To get a badge, ask the staff of the server you want it in.
</Warning>

Server managers can give out these badges:

* **Verified Account**
* **Government Account**
* **Business Account**
* **Elected Official**

An account can have only one of the **Verified**, **Government**, or **Business** checkmarks at a time. **Elected Official** can be added alongside any of them.

Accounts with a **Verified**, **Government**, or **Business** badge can also add [community notes](/features/internet#community-notes).

### Chirper Core Team

The **Chirper Core Team** badge marks accounts run by the team that builds and runs Chirper. If you see the orange Chirper bird next to a name, that account belongs to a member of the Chirper Core Team.

* The badge is added automatically to accounts owned by the Chirper Core Team.
* Server managers can't give it out or remove it.
* Anyone claiming to be on the Chirper Core Team without the badge isn't.

## Boosting and account limits

Boosting the [Chirper support server](https://chirper.app/support) raises your limit from 3 to **5 accounts** per server.

If your boost ends while you have more than 3 accounts in a server, Chirper DMs you. You then have **24 hours** to boost again.

<Warning>
  If you don't restore your boost within 24 hours, your extra accounts are **permanently deleted**.
</Warning>
